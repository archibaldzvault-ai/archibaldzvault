Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Archibaldz Vault ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

  • Email address — collected when you voluntarily sign up via our website opt-in form, to send you a welcome email sequence and occasional marketing updates.
  • Payment information — collected and processed entirely by Lemon Squeezy at checkout. We do not store your card details or payment information on our own servers.
  • Basic analytics data — we use Google Analytics to understand general site traffic and visitor behavior (e.g., pages visited, general location, device type). This data is aggregated and not used to personally identify you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver purchased digital products and related customer communication.
  • To send you the email welcome sequence and occasional marketing emails, if you've opted in.
  • To understand and improve website performance via analytics.

Third Parties We Work With

  • Lemon Squeezy — our payment processor and Merchant of Record, which handles all payment data securely.
  • MailerLite — our email service provider, used to send opt-in email communications.
  • Google Analytics — used for basic, aggregated website traffic analysis.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf.

Your Rights

If you are located in the UK or EU, you have rights under GDPR, including the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw your consent to marketing emails at any time
  • Object to how your data is processed

US-based visitors have similar rights under applicable state privacy laws.

How to Unsubscribe or Request Data Deletion

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email, or by contacting us at contact@archibaldzvault.com. To request access to or deletion of your data, please email us at the same address.

Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed, or until you request deletion.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@archibaldzvault.com.
