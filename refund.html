Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase from Archibaldz Vault. Because our products are digital and delivered instantly upon purchase, please review this policy before buying.

General Policy

Due to the instant, downloadable nature of digital products, all sales are generally final once a product has been delivered/accessed.

When a Refund May Be Considered

We will consider a refund request if:

  • You experience a technical issue that prevents you from accessing or downloading your purchased product, and our team is unable to resolve it within 14 days.
  • You were charged in error (e.g., duplicate charge for the same product).
  • The product delivered is significantly different from what was described at the time of purchase.

When a Refund Will Not Be Granted

  • Simply changing your mind after downloading or accessing the product.
  • Failure to read the product description before purchasing.
  • Requests made after 14 days from the date of purchase.

How to Request a Refund

Email contact@archibaldzvault.com with your order number and the reason for your request within 14 days of purchase. We aim to respond within 5 business days.

Processing

Approved refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy, our payment processor, and may take 5 business days to appear on your original payment method, depending on your bank or card provider.
